Dairya Aur Mehnat Ka Asli Arth

Ek baar ek sculptor (murtikar) ek pathar par chheni aur hathode se vaar kar raha tha. Pathar andar se cheekh utha aur kehne laga, "Mujhe mat maro, bohot dard ho raha hai!" Murtikar ne uss pathar ko chhod diya aur doosra pathar uthaya. Doosre pathar ne har vaar saha aur chup raha. Kuch dino baad wo ek shaandar murti ban gaya.

Jab mandir bana, toh pehla pathar jisne dard nahi saha tha, wo bahar fash par nariyal phodne ke kaam aaya, aur doosra pathar mandir ke andar bhagwan ban kar pooja gaya.

💡 Life Lesson: Aaj ka struggle aur dard hi kal aapko ek mazboot aur safal insaan banata hai.
